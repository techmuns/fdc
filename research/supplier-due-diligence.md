# FDC Supplier Due-Diligence — web + news verification

**Purpose:** before we recommend moving volume *to* a supplier or relying on one, verify them with real, multi-angle research. Every finding carries a real source (URL + date). Where nothing reliable exists we write "not found," never a guess.

**Scope (20 in-recommendation suppliers):** the ones that appear in a recommendation — overpayers, move-to targets, pay-too-soon (terms), and old-stock.

**Angles searched per supplier (open + keyword):** (1) capacity & expansion, material-specific; (2) financial health / credit rating; (3) risk & adverse news; (4) commercial fit (pharma/food-grade, certs, clients); (5) anything else the open search surfaces. Each ends with a one-line analyst verdict.

---

## Shrinath Roto Pack Pvt Ltd (SRPL) — *the founder's benchmark example*
**Role:** move-to target for **laminates** (FDC currently ~₹6.47 Cr/yr with them; the tool suggests shifting GEEP laminate volume here).
**Status:** unlisted (debt is rated; flagship of the family-owned "Shree group," 50+ yrs; incorporated 2006).

**Capacity & expansion (laminate-specific) — the founder's angle, with depth:**
- Cumulative **57,540 MTPA** across two Hyderabad plants — Mankhal (28,200) + Burgul (29,340) — plus in-house cylinder engraving (~25,000 cylinders/yr). Grew from ~42,000 MTPA (FY22) → 57,540 (FY24).
- Group spent **~₹430 Cr over 4 years** on SRPL expansion **and a greenfield aluminium-foil plant** (Nath Foils / NFPL, 14,600 MTPA, Aurangabad, commercial ops **Mar 2024**), ~₹320 Cr debt + promoter-funded.
- **No major capex planned near term** (CARE); strategy is to add capacity once utilisation hits ~80%.
- Source: [CARE Ratings press release, 9 Oct 2024](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202410151004_SHRINATH_ROTO_PACK_PRIVATE_LIMITED.pdf)

**Financial health (CARE, 9 Oct 2024):**
- Standalone SRPL Total Operating Income **₹1,113.88 Cr (FY23) → ₹1,018.92 Cr (FY24)** (slight dip); **PBDIT margin 8.65% (FY24, up from 6.79%)**, PAT margin 2.64%.
- **Overall gearing ~1.93x** standalone / 1.98x group FY24; interest coverage ~2.6x; **TD/GCA high at 10.1x** (debt-coverage flagged as moderate). Liquidity "adequate."
- So: a ₹1,000 Cr+ converter, thin (~2.6%) net margins (normal for packaging), **carrying meaningful expansion debt.**

**Credit rating (verified, cross-checked):**
- **CARE: BBB+ / Stable (9 Oct 2024)** — combined SRPL + Nath Foils view. [source](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202410151004_SHRINATH_ROTO_PACK_PRIVATE_LIMITED.pdf)
- **India Ratings: IND A- (Nov 2021) → downgraded to IND BBB+/Stable (Jan 2024)** on higher leverage from corporate guarantees to Nath Foils & Shrinath Flexipack. [upgrade](https://www.indiaratings.co.in/pressrelease?pressreleaseid=56650) · [downgrade](https://www.indiaratings.co.in/pressrelease/68159) *(agency pages are JS-rendered; dates per search index, rating corroborated by CARE)*
- **CRISIL: B/Stable "Issuer Not Cooperating" (press release 30 Aug 2024)** — SRPL stopped cooperating with CRISIL (confirmed inside the CARE release). A transparency/governance amber flag.

**Risk / adverse news:**
- Live drag = the **aluminium-foil venture (Nath Foils)** running at only ~40–50% capacity, revenue reportedly −55% in FY24 → the group is digesting that debt.
- Competitive, fragmented industry (low pricing power); raw-material (polymer) price swings, partly mitigated by pass-through pricing.
- No insolvency / fraud / plant-fire / recall found in the sweep.

**Commercial fit (strong):**
- Pharma-capable: makes multilayer laminates for FMCG + **pharma**; marquee clients **Britannia, P&G, GSK** (top-10 customers = ~36% of FY24 income). Certs **BRC-IOP, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015**; exports to 25+ countries. [capacity/certs](https://packagingsouthasia.com/) · [brochure](https://www.slplindia.com/)

**ANALYST VERDICT — *viable second source + price lever, not sole-source (yet).*** Capacity and pharma-grade quality are **not** the gap the founder feared: at 57,540 MTPA and serving GSK/P&G/Britannia, they can clearly take FDC's laminate volume. **But** the group is carrying expansion debt (gearing ~2x, high TD/GCA), took a Jan-2024 India Ratings downgrade, sits under a CRISIL non-cooperation flag, and is nursing an underperforming foil plant. So: use them to **dual-source and push GEEP's price down**, move **incremental** volume now, and verify current utilisation headroom before making them a sole-source for critical laminates. **Confidence: High** (CARE primary doc + India Ratings + CRISIL cross-checked).
